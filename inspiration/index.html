01

Fresh photo collage

Photo-led and busy. Real leaves and produce are cut out and pushed over the section edges, with faint food doodles behind.

The one that stands apart: more commercial and photo-led than everything else here.

  • Light green-white page
  • Cut-out photos of greens and produce breaking the frame
  • Faint line-art food doodles in the background
  • Many small sections, cards and buttons

Take for Atulyash

  • Wheat ears and grains crossing section edges
  • A faint line-drawing layer behind the quiet sections

Skip

  • Card grids full of placeholder text
  • The black discount countdown
  • Star ratings
  • Three identical "Order now" tiles
02

Product-first D2C

The pack is the hero. It sits big, in its raw ingredients, with one line and one button.

The most direct route to "this is a real product you can buy".

  • Pack as the largest thing on screen
  • Raw ingredients around the pack
  • A solid colour panel beside it
  • Clean commercial grid

Take for Atulyash

  • Our 2 kg pack big, with wheat and brown atta around it
  • A solid olive panel holding plain facts: milled the day before, stone chakki, chokar kept in
  • One product, one line, one button on the first screen

Skip

  • Partner logo bar
  • Invented numbers like "500M+ happy clients"
  • Uppercase eyebrows
  • The orange button
03

Dark immersive

Full-bleed deep green, a hyperreal pile of produce and one giant word with a ghost word behind it.

Strong, but heavy for older readers if used everywhere. Best as one dark moment.

  • Deep green full-bleed ground
  • Hyperreal, glossy produce cluster
  • One oversized display word
  • Ghost type behind the hero

Take for Atulyash

  • The scale: one huge word ("Atta" or "purest") with the pack and a heap of wheat
  • One dark section, for example the close

Skip

  • The "AI-powered" label
  • A dark page throughout
  • Glassy chips and floating icons
04

Botanical heritage

Cream and deep green, fine botanical line drawings, warm wood and jute in soft daylight. Calm and crafted.

Closest to how the Atulyash pack itself already feels.

  • Cream with a deep green band
  • Fine botanical line drawings
  • Warm wood, jute, daylight
  • Serif and sans paired

Take for Atulyash

  • Cream and deep green as the base
  • The jute sack and wooden surfaces in photos
  • A botanical wheat drawing as a line accent
  • Quiet white sections around the journey

Skip

  • "100% organic" badges and claims
  • Tiny type
05

Grainy flat illustration

Flat shapes with grain texture, cropped by big circles, with a friendly bold sans and a numbered row.

The same family as our style 7 journey, so it joins the page to the journey.

  • Flat shapes with speckled grain
  • Drawings cropped by big circles
  • Friendly bold sans headline
  • Numbered 01/02/03 row with one coloured block

Take for Atulyash

  • Circle-cropped style 7 spot drawings for each question and step
  • The numbered row for how the weekly plan works
  • Small drawings instead of stock icons
  • One plain freshness line with a drawing and lots of space

Skip

  • The coral background
  • Animal characters (our only character is Veer, from supplied art)
  • Neon green and farming claims
06

Editorial utility

Plain, useful patterns: tick lists, food-first photos and big type over the product.

Patterns to borrow, not a look to copy.

  • Short rows with clear ticks
  • Food shown before process
  • Big type moments over the product

Take for Atulyash

  • The six questions as tick rows you tap yourself
  • Show what the atta becomes: a puffed roti at the end of the journey

Skip

  • Any "us versus them" comparison column
  • Shouty all-caps headings
  • Chapter dots for the journey (you chose a continuous loop)
07

Avoid

What tires an older reader: many things asking for attention at once.

Collected from every style above.

  • Discount strips and sale popups
  • Badges, ratings and logo bars
  • Many tiles of equal weight
  • Chat bubbles and floating icons

Take for Atulyash

  • No popups, no discount strips, one clear next step per screen

Skip

  • Placeholder text and invented numbers
  • Uppercase eyebrows and shouty headings
  • Comparison with other brands
  • Organic or health claims
08

Field photo hero

A full-bleed wheat photo sets the palette and carries large white type.

Both concepts use golden wheat to give the opening its colour.

  • Full-bleed wheat photo with blue sky or a soft-focus close-up
  • Large white sans-serif type on the left or centred
  • Round lime button crossing the photo edge in WFARM
  • Frosted-glass wordmark pill and a row of thin line icons in SageWheat

Take for Atulyash

  • Set one large white headline over a quiet part of the wheat photo
  • Use a deep green panel to anchor a short row of plain facts
  • Use soft focus behind white type and keep any line icons paired with short, factual labels

Skip

  • The translucent stat card over the wheat
  • Farm acreage and harvest totals
  • The second competing action in the navigation
  • Organic and health claims such as "100% organic" and "nourishing goodness"
