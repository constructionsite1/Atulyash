The Atulyash way

Six decisions. One honest packet.

  1. 1

    One wheat

    Single-origin MP Sharbati. Never blended with other varieties.

  2. 2

    Cleaned in stages

    Multi-stage cleaning before the grain reaches the chakki.

  3. 3

    Natural stone

    Natural stone chakkis. No emery stone, no steel rollers.

  4. 4

    Milled slowly

    Slow and gentle, to keep heat down.

  5. 5

    Chokar kept in

    Nothing removed, nothing added. Packed with minimal handling.

  6. 6

    To your door

    Milled in small batches the day before, delivered the next morning.
