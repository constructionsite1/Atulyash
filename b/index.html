The Atulyash way

Six decisions. One honest packet.

  1. 01 · The wheat

    One wheat, one origin

    Single-origin MP Sharbati. Never blended with other varieties.

  2. 02 · The cleaning

    Cleaned in stages

    Multi-stage cleaning, so only grain reaches the stone.

  3. 03 · The mill

    Natural stone

    Natural stone chakkis. No emery stone, no steel rollers.

  4. 04 · The speed

    Slowly

    The stone turns slowly, keeping heat down and the grain's character in.

  5. 05 · The chokar

    Nothing taken out

    Chokar kept in. Small batches, packed with minimal handling.

  6. 06 · The freshness

    To your door

    Milled the day before, delivered the next morning, every week.
